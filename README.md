# Yashi & Siddhant — Wedding Invitation 💍

A single-page wedding invitation website with:

- A **tap-to-open envelope video** intro (`assets/intro.mp4` for desktop, `assets/intro-mobile.mp4` portrait version for phones)
- A black & gold "Save The Date" card with the couple's names and the date
- A live countdown to the wedding
- Event cards (Haldi, Sangeet, Wedding…) with **Directions** and **Add to Calendar** buttons
- An RSVP form that sends the response to you on WhatsApp
- A layout that works on phones and computers

## Customise it

Open **`script.js`** and edit the `WEDDING` object at the top: names, parents, dates, venues, map links, your WhatsApp number and contacts. You don't need to touch the HTML.

## Preview locally

Open `index.html` in any browser.

## Publish it for free (GitHub Pages)

1. Go to the repository's **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
3. After a minute, your invitation will be live at
   `https://rachitchaturvedi1997.github.io/Wedding_card/`. Share that link with your guests.
